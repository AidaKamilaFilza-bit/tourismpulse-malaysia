From disruption to recovery.

2019
PRE-PANDEMIC

The benchmark year

2020–21
DISRUPTION

Tourism activity falls sharply

Travel restrictions and reduced mobility disrupted tourism activity across Malaysian destinations.

2022

As mobility returned, tourism activity began rebuilding across the country.

2023
RECOVERY

Not every destination recovered equally

By 2023, some destinations had returned to or exceeded their 2019 tourism levels, while others remained below their pre-pandemic benchmark.

METHODOLOGY NOTE

How to interpret the results

Tourism recovery is measured relative to the 2019 pre-pandemic benchmark. Local price comparisons use matched tourism areas and comparison towns across selected staple categories.

Price differences are descriptive. They show observed differences between selected locations and should not be interpreted as evidence that tourism caused changes in local prices.
